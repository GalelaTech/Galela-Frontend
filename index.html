Scientific Research & Laboratory Support

We provide practical scientific and laboratory support to students, researchers, startups and small businesses working in chemistry, materials science, natural products and formulation research.

From sample preparation and extraction to laboratory testing, materials characterisation, data organisation and scientific reporting, we help turn your defined experimental requirements into a structured laboratory workflow.

Experimental Preparation

Planning, setup, and practical laboratory organisation.

Sample Preparation

Extraction and preparation of natural-product samples.

Microbiology & Screening

Antimicrobial, DPPH, anti-tyrosinase and anti-elastase assays.

Characterisation

FTIR, XRD, SEM, TEM, TGA and DSC analysis support.

Cosmetic & Formulation

Formulation research support for cosmetic applications.

Data & Reporting

Calculations, graphs, tables, interpretation and documentation.

About Galela Solutions

A multidisciplinary company built on science, delivered through technology — offering research, digital, transport, and property solutions to clients across South Africa.

Founder & CEO

Isaac X Galela

A scientist by training, a technologist by practice. Isaac founded Galela Solutions to bring rigorous, practical support to researchers, businesses, and individuals who need reliable help — without the overhead.

He began at the University of Pretoria studying BSc Chemistry, then worked at CSIR as a Cosmetic Lab Technician and Materials Characteriser. Alongside this, he's continued building technology skills through the FNB App Academy, freeCodeCamp, and Google AWS — and is now branching into CompTIA+.
